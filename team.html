OUR TEAM

2025-2026 Season

Ascension Robotics is a student design team affiliated with Simon Fraser University that has been participating in the ARC Robotics Competition for 3 years. Our team consists of 30 members pursuing degrees in mechatronics, computer science, business, and more.

Together, our multi-disciplinary engineers design, build, and deploy competitive robotic systems from the ground up, pushing the boundaries of autonomous navigation, high-speed mechanisms, and robust embedded hardware.
